# Prompts para GPT Image 2 — mockup de la remera

GPT Image 2 renderiza texto muy bien, así que estos prompts piden el texto directo.
Texto exacto: **YOU ARE THE AD** · Tipografía: **Montserrat ExtraBold** · va **espejado**.

> Si en alguna corrida una letra del espejado sale torcida, regenerá (sale distinto cada
> vez) o usá el truco infalible: generá con el texto **legible** y das vuelta la imagen en
> horizontal (flip). Para el archivo final de impresión, hacelo vectorial (ver `PROMPT-REMERA.md`).

---

## 1) Mockup principal (ghost mannequin, fondo negro) ← el bueno

```
Premium e-commerce product mockup of a black heavyweight cotton t-shirt, shown on an
invisible ghost mannequin, front view, perfectly centered, on a seamless deep matte black
studio background (#0A0A0E). Soft directional key light from the upper left, subtle rim
light along the shoulders and sleeves, realistic cotton texture with gentle natural
wrinkles.

On the chest, printed small and centered in bold white Montserrat ExtraBold, two lines:
  line 1: YOU ARE
  line 2: THE AD

IMPORTANT: the printed text is MIRROR-REVERSED — a horizontal flip, as if seen in a mirror.
Every letter is reversed left-to-right; the two lines stay stacked top to bottom. Crisp,
high-contrast white-on-black print, tight letter spacing.

Mood: eerie, minimalist, premium streetwear; cold desaturated cinematic color grade like the
Netflix series "Dark". Ultra sharp, photorealistic, studio quality, high detail, 4k.
No people, no logos, no extra text.
```

## 2) Plano flat-lay (remera apoyada, cenital)

```
Top-down flat-lay photo of a black heavyweight cotton t-shirt neatly laid on a textured
matte black surface, soft even studio light, subtle shadows, realistic fabric folds.
Centered on the chest area, in white Montserrat ExtraBold, two lines "YOU ARE" / "THE AD",
printed MIRROR-REVERSED (horizontally flipped, like a mirror reflection), crisp white-on-
black, tight tracking. Minimal, premium, cinematic cold color grade like the series "Dark".
Photorealistic, sharp, 4k, no people, no extra text.
```

## 3) La foto-concepto (persona frente al espejo) — la que más vende

```
Cinematic photograph of a person standing in front of a large mirror in a dark, moody room,
seen from behind so we see their back and their reflection in the mirror. They wear a black
t-shirt. In the mirror reflection, the chest text reads correctly and legibly: "YOU ARE THE
AD" in white Montserrat ExtraBold. Low-key dramatic lighting, cold desaturated color grade
like the Netflix series "Dark", subtle film grain, shallow depth of field. Photorealistic,
4k.
```

> Tamaño de salida sugerido: cuadrado 1024×1024 para el mockup del sitio, o vertical
